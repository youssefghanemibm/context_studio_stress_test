## Welcome

Welcome to Programme Skerry. Corvane Mutual is replacing CLARA, its mainframe claims system (COBOL,
CICS and DB2, live since 1994), with Claims Hub, a set of Java/Spring Boot microservices on
OpenShift. The IBM engagement started on 13 January 2025.

## Systems you will hear about

- CLARA – Core claims administration (legacy)
- Claims Hub – Core claims administration (target)
- FRAUDNET – Fraud scoring engine
- DOCSTOR – Document and image store
- Adjuster Extranet – Vendor assessor portal
- MyCorvane Claims – Customer self-service claims portal

The Hub squads (Intake & Triage, Reserve & Finance, Fraud & Settlement) were formed on 3 February
2025 and report to Neil Haworth, Head of Technology Delivery.
