## Where we are

UK motor claims moved from CLARA to Claims Hub at the Wave 1 cut-over on Monday 14 September 2026.

## Rules you will use every day

- Acknowledge digital FNOLs within 1 business day and all other channels within 2 business days
  (BR-FNOL-001 v3).
- Payment authority: Claims Handler £15,000; Team Leader £75,000 (BR-STL-002 v3).
- Reserve authority: Claims Handler £25,000; Team Leader £100,000 (BR-RSV-003 v3).
- Fraud referral: claims with a Sentinel score of 65 or more go to SIU; from 1 October 2026 Tier-3
  claims go at 60 or more.
- Never decline or reduce a claim because of the score alone – SIU review is always required
  (BR-FRD-033).

## Systems around Claims Hub

- Evidence Vault – Document and image store
- Assessor Portal – Vendor assessor portal
- Sentinel – Fraud scoring engine
- MyCorvane Claims – Customer self-service claims portal
- Corvane Payments Gateway – Payments

## Getting help

- Floor-walkers are on the Leeds floor during hypercare.
- Raise system questions in the hypercare channel with a claim reference, never customer details.
- Team leader: Callum Fairbairn.

## Tips from the first week

- If an assessor report is missing, check the Assessor Portal status first.
- Documents are in Evidence Vault – open them from the claim, not by searching.
- Save notes as you go; the session times out after inactivity.
- Check the work queue filter before assuming a claim is missing.
