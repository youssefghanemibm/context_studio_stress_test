Notes byOskar Lindgren, Sentinel model owner

Purpose

Review the calibration of Sentinel model SNT-4.2, completed on 16 March 2026, ahead of the change of referral threshold to 65 on 1 April 2026 (BR-FRD-031 v1), and agree the evidence needed for paragraph 3.3 of NISA Circular 2026/04.

Background

Sentinel scores every claim at FNOL and triage on a scale from 0 to 100. The current model, SNT-4.2, is a gradient-boosted model supplied with the licensed scoring engine; Oskar Lindgren is the model owner and Niall Brannigan the business owner of Sentinel.

From 1 April 2026 NISA Circular 2026/04 requires score-based cut-offs to be calibrated and documented. The calibration reviewed today is the evidence for the 65 cut-off that replaces the current 70.

Method

  1. Score the calibration sample with SNT-4.2 as deployed in production.
  2. Compare the share of scored claims that would be referred at the current and the new threshold.
  3. Check the outcome against SIU triage capacity and the requirements of the Circular.
  4. Record the result and the sign-offs in the model documentation.

Discussion

  • Ruth Okafor confirmed that paragraph 3.3 requires score-based cut-offs to be calibrated and documented, and that on a 0–100 scale the cut-off must not exceed 65; these notes and the calibration pack form part of that evidence.
  • Rachel Dunmore noted that from 1 April SIU must triage new referrals within 3 business days (BR-FRD-035 v2) and asked for weekly referral volumes in the first month.
  • Independent validation of the model by Risk is booked for Friday 20 March 2026.
  • Linnea Sjöberg confirmed the threshold is a configuration value in fraud-referral-svc and will be set for 1 April in line with CLARA.
  • Niall Brannigan asked that the calibration pack is filed with the model documentation before go-live.
