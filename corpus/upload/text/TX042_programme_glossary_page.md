# Programme Skerry – Glossary

| | |
|---|---|
| **Page owner** | Hannah Lyle, Business Analyst – Claims (IBM Consulting) |
| **Last reviewed** | 15 July 2026 |
| **Audience** | Everyone working on Programme Skerry |

This page lists the business terms used across the programme, with the variants you will see in manuals, slides, spreadsheets, emails and code. Use the canonical term in new documents.

| Term | Meaning | Also seen as |
|---|---|---|
| Bordereau | Periodic listing of claims ceded to reinsurers. | bordereaux, bdx |
| CLARA | Claims Ledger And Reserve Application (legacy mainframe). | the ledger, CLR, Claims Ledger, the mainframe |
| Claim Type | The peril or cause of loss classifying a claim (e.g. Escape of Water). | peril, cause of loss, loss type |
| Claims Assessor | Person (internal employee or panel vendor) who inspects or desk-assesses a loss and reports an estimate and cover view. | loss adjuster, adjuster, assessor, claims adjuster, skadetakstmann, besiktningsman, Adjuster Extranet |
| Claims Handler | Employee who manages a claim day to day. | claim handler, case handler, handler |
| Claims Hub | The target claims platform. | CHUB, the Hub |
| Counter-Fraud & SIU | The Special Investigations Unit (renamed Counter-Fraud & SIU on 2025-09-01). | SIU, Special Investigations Unit, counter-fraud team |
| Customer Referral Notice | Written notice to the customer that a claim is subject to additional checks (BR-FRD-032). | fraud notice, referral notice, notice of additional checks |
| Excess | The amount the policyholder bears on each claim. | deductible, egenandel, självrisk, selvrisiko |
| First Notice of Loss | The first notification of a claim by any channel. | FNOL, notification of loss, first notification, skademelding |
| Fraud Referral | Referral of a claim to the Counter-Fraud & SIU team for investigation. | SIU referral, referral to SIU, fraud referral, referral |
| Fraud Score | Sentinel score from 0 to 100. | Sentinel score, FRAUDNET score, risk score, SNT score |
| Incurred | Paid to date plus outstanding reserve. | incurred cost, total incurred |
| Large Loss Notification Threshold | £250,000 reserve movement threshold for notifying Reinsurance (BR-RSV-011 v2). | LLNT, large loss limit |
| Policyholder | Holder of the policy (a member of the mutual). | insured, member, customer |
| Recovery & Subrogation Process Owner | Accountable owner of process stage S7. | Recoveries Manager, Head of Recoveries & Subrogation |
| Reserve | Case reserve: the estimated outstanding cost of a claim, per reserve type. | case reserve, case estimate, outstanding, RSV, reserve table |
| Reserve Movement | A change to a reserve, recorded in CLR.T_RESERVE_MOVEMENT / reserve.reserve_movement. | reserve change, reserve adjustment, movement |
| Salvage | Disposal of damaged property (mainly total-loss vehicles) to recover value. | salvage disposal, write-off category |
| Settlement | Agreement and payment of the claim. | claim payment, indemnity payment |
| Subrogation | Recovery of claim outlay from a responsible third party or its insurer. | sub-ro, subro, recovery, recoveries, regress |
| Technical Provisions | Claims outstanding provisions reported to NISA in the TP2 return. | TPs, claims outstanding provisions |
| Tier-3 Claim | A claim meeting the Tier-3 definition in BR-TRI-003 (v3 from 2025-01-01). | T3, complex claim, major & complex loss |


## Abbreviations

- CAB – Change Advisory Board
- ADR – Architecture Decision Record
- BRD – Business Requirements Document
- NISA – National Insurance Supervisory Authority
- PMO – Programme Management Office
- SME – Subject-matter expert
