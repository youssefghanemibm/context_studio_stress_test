Morning Gemma,

The current threshold is £750: we pursue subrogation where the
recoverable amount is £750 or more (BR-REC-003).

The £500 figure was the old rule – it was replaced in 2022.

If a case is borderline, or further costs are likely to come through,
refer it anyway and we will take a view.
