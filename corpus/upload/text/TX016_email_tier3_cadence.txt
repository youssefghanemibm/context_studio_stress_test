Morning Rob,

Confirmed: from 1 July 2026, reserves on Tier-3 claims must be reviewed
at least every 14 days (BR-RSV-007 v3, approved by the Reserving
Committee on 9 June).

Until 30 June the current 21-day cadence still applies.

Daniel and Keith are copied because the rule is implemented in both
reserve-engine and CLARA (CLRRS110 and CLRRS120), so both need the new
interval from 1 July.

Kirsty's team will feel this most, so I have copied her too.
