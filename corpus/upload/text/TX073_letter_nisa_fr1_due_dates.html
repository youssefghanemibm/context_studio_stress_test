NISA Circular 2026/04 – Quarterly Fraud Referral Return (NISA-FR1): submission dates

I am writing to confirm the submission arrangements for the quarterly NISA-FR1 fraud referral return introduced by paragraph 3.4 of NISA Circular 2026/04, Fair Treatment in Fraud Referral, which took effect on 1 April 2026.

The return is due within 30 calendar days after the end of each calendar quarter. Your firm's first return covers the quarter from 1 April to 30 June 2026 (2026Q2) and is due by 30 July 2026.

The following return, for 2026Q3, will be due by 30 October 2026. Returns should be submitted through the NISA reporting portal in the published XML format.

We note that the return will be signed by the firm's Compliance Manager – Conduct & Counter-Fraud, and that you will be the named signatory for Corvane Mutual.

Please also note that paragraph 3.5 of the Circular requires referral records to be retained for 7 years, and that the data in each return should be reconcilable to those records.

If you expect any difficulty in meeting a submission date, please contact me before the due date so that we can discuss it. Questions on the policy intent of the Circular may be directed to my colleague Petter Ulvestad, who is copied.
