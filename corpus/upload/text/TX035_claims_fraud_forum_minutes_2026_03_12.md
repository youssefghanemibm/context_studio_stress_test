## 2. NISA Circular 2026/04 – Fair Treatment in Fraud Referral

Ruth Okafor summarised NISA Circular 2026/04, published on 20 November 2025 and effective from 1 April 2026. Its main requirements are:

- §3.1 – no claim may be declined, reduced or repudiated on an automated score alone; a documented human review is required;
- §3.2 – the customer must be told in writing within 10 business days of referral that the claim is subject to additional checks, with a deferral possible where a tipping-off risk is certified;
- §3.3 – score-based cut-offs must be calibrated and documented, and on a 0–100 scale must not exceed 65;
- §3.4 – a quarterly NISA-FR1 fraud referral return within 30 days after each quarter end;
- §3.5 – referral records retained for 7 years.

Hannah Lyle confirmed that the approved fraud-referral BRD (v1.0) maps each requirement to a rule and a system change in CLARA and Claims Hub.

## 4. Superseded and withdrawn rules

- BR-FRD-012 v2 (referral at a score of 70 or more) is superseded by BR-FRD-031 from 1 April 2026.
- BR-FRD-019 (automatic decline on a very high score) is withdrawn from 1 April 2026, because §3.1 prohibits adverse decisions on an automated score alone.
