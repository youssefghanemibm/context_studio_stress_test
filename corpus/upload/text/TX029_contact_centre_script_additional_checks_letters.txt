WHY CUSTOMERS ARE CALLING
  From 1 April 2026 we write to customers within 10 business days when their claim is selected for additional checks.
  Some customers will be worried by the letter. Your job is to reassure them, not to explain the checks.

WHAT TO SAY
  - "Thank you for calling. I can see the letter you've received. These checks are a normal part of how we handle some claims."
  - "It doesn't mean we've made any decision about your claim."
  - "Your handler will contact you if they need anything else from you."
  - If asked: "No decision on a claim is ever made on an automated check alone. A person always reviews it."

WHAT NOT TO SAY
  - Do not use the words 'fraud', 'investigation' or 'SIU'.
  - Do not mention scores or how claims are selected.
  - Do not give a date for the outcome of the checks.
  - Do not transfer the call to the SIU team.

RECORDING THE CALL
  Log the call in Harbour CRM with wrap code ADDCHK-QUERY and add a short note for the handler.
  If the customer wants to complain, log a complaint in the usual way and tell your team leader.
