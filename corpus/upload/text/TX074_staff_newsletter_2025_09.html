A new shape for Claims, Risk and Finance

By Duncan Ferrier, Chief Operating Officer

The re-organisation of our Claims, Risk & Compliance and Finance functions took effect on Monday 1 September 2025. Here is what changed and who is now leading what.

Recoveries & Subrogation becomes a Group function, led by Freya Lindahl as Head of Recoveries & Subrogation. Freya reports to the Claims Director, Ingrid Ashby (née Sørensen), and owns the recovery and subrogation process for every region from 1 September.

Our Special Investigations Unit becomes Counter-Fraud & SIU and moves from Claims into Risk & Compliance. Niall Brannigan becomes Head of Counter-Fraud & SIU, reporting to Karin Holmberg, Chief Risk Officer.

Siobhan Quill, who has led Recoveries as Acting Recoveries Manager since January, becomes Recoveries Team Leader – UK, reporting to Freya. Thank you, Siobhan, for steering the team through the year.

Adjuster Extranet is now Assessor Portal

Claims Assessment UK

From 1 September 2025 the Adjuster Extranet is called Assessor Portal, so that the system name matches the role we now use everywhere: Claims Assessor. Nothing changes for vendor assessors beyond the name on screen; the change was delivered under CHUB-157.

Questions to Imran Talwar's team in Claims Assessment UK.

Welcome to our new CFO

Executive Office

Priya Raman joined Corvane as Chief Financial Officer on 1 July 2025, succeeding Gareth Whitcombe, who left at the end of June after six years as CFO. Priya leads finance, reserving, actuarial, treasury and payments.

Programme Skerry: Claims Hub MVP on track for November

Programme Skerry team

The first release of Claims Hub – first notice of loss and triage for our digital channels – is planned to go live on 3 November 2025. Claims Hub will replace CLARA in waves, with CLARA remaining the system of record for claims that have not yet moved.

Look out for demo sessions in Leeds, Glasgow and London over the coming weeks.

New authority limits and recovery threshold from 1 September

Claims Standards

Revised reserve and payment authority limits (BR-RSV-003 v3 and BR-STL-002 v3) apply from 1 September 2025. For example, a Claims Handler can now authorise payments up to £15,000 and a Team Leader up to £75,000.

The subrogation pursuit threshold also rises: from 1 September we pursue subrogation where the recoverable amount is £1,000 or more (BR-REC-003 v3).
