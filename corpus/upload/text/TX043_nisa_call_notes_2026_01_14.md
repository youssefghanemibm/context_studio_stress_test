## Purpose

Corvane asked for the call to clarify implementation questions on NISA Circular 2026/04 ahead of its effective date of 1 April 2026.

## Background

The Circular was published on 20 November 2025. Corvane's implementation is led by Compliance, with Counter-Fraud & SIU owning the referral process and the fraud-referral BRD covering system changes in CLARA and Claims Hub.

## Notes

- Written notice (§3.2): we asked whether an SMS can count as the written notice to the customer. Petter Ulvestad confirmed that an SMS counts as written notice under §3.2 if it is followed by a letter within 2 business days.
- Cut-off (§3.3): NISA expects firms to keep the calibration evidence for any score-based cut-off, and reminded us that on a 0–100 scale the cut-off must not exceed 65.
- FR1 return (§3.4): the return is quarterly, within 30 days after quarter end. Questions on submission mechanics should go to our supervisor, Imogen Thackray.
- No adverse decision on score alone (§3.1): Niall Brannigan outlined that SIU review is documented for every adverse outcome; no further comment from NISA.
