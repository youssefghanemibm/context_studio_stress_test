## 1. Opening, minutes and actions

The chair opened the meeting, welcomed attendees and confirmed quorum. No declarations of interest

The minutes of the last meeting were agreed as a true record. The action log was reviewed. Closed
items have been removed; carried-forward items are listed below.

## 2. Reserve parallel run – progress

The Committee received an update on the reserve parallel run between CLARA and reserve-engine, which
started on 2 March 2026 and is planned to end on 28 August 2026 (CHUB-241). Daily comparisons are
reviewed by the Reserving team with the Reserve & Finance squad, and differences are explained
before the next day's run.

The Committee noted the update on audit finding IA-25-07: as agreed at the reserving design review
on 5 March 2026, reserve-engine will record currency revaluations as RV movements, which closes the
finding in the target platform, while CLRGL090 stays unchanged in CLARA.

In discussion the following points were made:

- The chair thanked the team for the clarity of the paper.

## 3. Tier-3 reserve review cadence (BR-RSV-007)

The chair presented the proposal from the reserving design review of 5 March 2026 to shorten the
review interval for reserves on Tier-3 claims. Under BR-RSV-007 v2, in force since 1 January 2024,
Tier-3 reserves must be reviewed at least every 21 days.

In discussion:

- Kirsty Mowbray confirmed that Major & Complex Loss would plan its review diary around the shorter
  interval and asked for the start date to align with a month boundary.
- Sebastian Moreau-Clarke supported the change as consistent with the reserving policy.
- Hamish Gilchrist asked that month-end reporting reflects the change from the first affected close.
- Ingrid Ashby supported the proposal on behalf of Claims.

Decision: the Committee approved BR-RSV-007 v3 – reserves on Tier-3 claims must be reviewed at least
every 14 days – effective 1 July 2026. Approved by the chair, Lena Kristiansen.

## 4. Large loss notifications

The Committee noted the large loss notifications made to Reinsurance since the last meeting under
BR-RSV-011 v2, which requires notification to ReCede within 24 hours of any reserve movement
exceeding the Large Loss Notification Threshold (LLNT) of £250,000. No late notifications were
reported.

## 5. Wave 0 (Denmark)

The Committee noted that the Wave 0 cut-over for Denmark took place on 1 June 2026, covering all
Danish products (PRD-MOT-DK and PRD-HOM-DK). Hamish Gilchrist confirmed the first month-end close
after the cut-over will be reviewed with the Reserving team.

  Status: approved 12 December 2023 by Sebastian Moreau-Clarke, Per Øverland; effective from 1

- **BR-RSV-003 v3** – Reserve authority limits
  Text: Reserve authority: Claims Handler £25,000; Team Leader £100,000; Claims Manager £500,000;

  GBP equivalent at the GL month-end rate.
  Status: approved 12 August 2025 by Sebastian Moreau-Clarke, Ingrid Sørensen; effective from 1
  September 2025.
