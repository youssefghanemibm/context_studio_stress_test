Created by Niall Brannigan, last modified by Niall Brannigan on Oct 02, 2026

What changed on 2026-10-01

From 2026-10-01 Tier-3 claims are referred to SIU at a Sentinel score of 60 or more; all other claims stay at 65 (Fraud Rules Workshop 2026-09-17, DEC-014). This page replaces the April 2026 version.

Tier-3 definition

Who counts as Tier-3?

BR-TRI-003 v3: A claim is Tier-3 if any of: estimated incurred ≥ £50,000 (NOK 650,000 / SEK 680,000 / DKK 430,000); any bodily injury; solicitor or litigation involvement at notification; property total loss. Tier-2: £5,000–£49,999 or any third-party involvement. Tier-1: below £5,000, no injury, no third party.

Regulatory basis

ParagraphRequirement
3.1No claim may be declined, reduced or repudiated on the basis of an automated score alone; a documented human review is required.
3.2Tell the customer in writing within 10 business days of referral that the claim is subject to additional checks; the notice may be deferred for up to 60 days where a tipping-off risk is certified.
3.3Score-based referral cut-offs must be calibrated and documented; on a 0–100 scale the cut-off must not exceed 65.
3.4Submit the quarterly NISA-FR1 fraud referral return within 30 days after each quarter end.
3.5Retain referral records for 7 years.

FAQ

Does the Tier-3 threshold apply to claims referred before 2026-10-01?
No. The threshold in force on the scoring date applies.
Can a claim be declined because of a high score?
No. BR-FRD-033 requires a documented human review by SIU for every adverse outcome.
When must the customer be told?
Within 10 business days of the referral, unless the notice is deferred under BR-FRD-036.
Who defines Tier-3?
BR-TRI-003 v3 (Claims Operations).
