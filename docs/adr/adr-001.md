# ADR-001: Strangler-fig migration from CLARA to Claims Hub

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2025-02-10
- Deciders: Elin Fosse (Lead Solution Architect), Tomasz Wójcik (Enterprise Architect), Adaeze Nwosu (Chief Information Officer)
- Forum: Design Authority
- Supersedes: none
- Superseded by: none

## Context

CLARA has been the claims system of record since 1994 and every open claim lives in it. Replacing it in one cut-over would put the whole claims operation at risk at once.

## Decision

Replace CLARA capability by capability with Claims Hub services and move traffic to each new service as its capability and region migrate.

## Consequences

Migrate by capability and region in waves; CLARA remains the system of record for unmigrated claims until 2027-09-30.

## Notes

Questions about this decision go to the Design Authority agenda.
