# ADR-014: Customer notifications through notification-svc and Notify

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-01-26
- Deciders: Elin Fosse (Lead Solution Architect), Sofie Bak (Engineering Lead – Intake & Triage Squad), Ruth Okafor (Compliance Manager – Conduct & Counter-Fraud)
- Forum: Design Authority
- Supersedes: none
- Superseded by: none

## Context

Fraud referral notices have a regulatory deadline and must use approved wording.

## Decision

Send all customer notifications through notification-svc and the Notify platform.

## Consequences

Fraud-referral notices use Notify templates FRN01 (letter) and FRN02 (SMS).

## Notes

Teams record follow-up work in the Claims Hub backlog and link it to this record.
