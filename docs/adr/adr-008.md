# ADR-008: Workforce and partner identity through CIS (OIDC)

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2025-03-03
- Deciders: Tomasz Wójcik (Enterprise Architect), Neil Haworth (Head of Technology Delivery)
- Forum: Design Authority
- Supersedes: none
- Superseded by: none

## Context

Staff and partner users need one identity across Claims Hub and the partner portal.

## Decision

Authenticate workforce and partner users through Corvane Identity Service using OIDC.

## Consequences

Assessor Portal and Claims Hub use CIS.

## Notes

Teams record follow-up work in the Claims Hub backlog and link it to this record.
