# ADR-020: Fraud referral thresholds as effective-dated configuration

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2026-02-16
- Deciders: Elin Fosse (Lead Solution Architect), Linnea Sjöberg (Engineering Lead – Fraud & Settlement Squad), Niall Brannigan (Head of Counter-Fraud & SIU)
- Forum: Design Authority
- Supersedes: none
- Superseded by: none

## Context

NISA Circular 2026/04 and the fraud rules workshops change referral thresholds on fixed dates.

## Decision

Configure fraud referral thresholds as effective-dated values in fraud-referral-svc's configuration.

## Consequences

referral.threshold.default and referral.threshold.tier3 with effective-from dates.

## Notes

Questions about this decision go to the Design Authority agenda.
