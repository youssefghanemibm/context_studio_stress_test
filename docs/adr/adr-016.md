# ADR-016: Store Nordic amounts in original currency and GBP

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2025-06-23
- Deciders: Nadia Petrovic (Java Technical Lead), Lena Kristiansen (Reserving Manager)
- Forum: Design Authority
- Supersedes: none
- Superseded by: none

## Context

Nordic claims are paid and reserved in local currency, but authority checks and finance reporting work in GBP.

## Decision

Store every amount in its original currency and as a GBP equivalent.

## Consequences

Minor units (øre/pence) in events.

## Notes

Implementation details live in the service repositories and are not repeated here.
