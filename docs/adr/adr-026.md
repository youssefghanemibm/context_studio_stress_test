# ADR-026: Read-only CLARA enquiry after cut-over

- Status: Proposed
- Date: 2026-05-11
- Deciders: Graham Pocock (Head of Legacy Claims Platforms), Elin Fosse (Lead Solution Architect)
- Forum: Design Authority
- Supersedes: none
- Superseded by: none

## Context

Claims handlers still need to look up history in CLARA after their claims have migrated.

## Decision

Keep CICS enquiry transactions available read-only after cut-over until CLARA is decommissioned.

## Consequences

CICS enquiry transactions kept read-only until decommissioning.

> Proposed: not yet agreed by the Design Authority.

## Notes

Changes to this decision need a new ADR reviewed by the Design Authority.
