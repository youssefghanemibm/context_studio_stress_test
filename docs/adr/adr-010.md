# ADR-010: Business rules as versioned configuration, not a rules engine

- Status: Accepted
- Date: 2025-04-07
- Deciders: Nadia Petrovic (Java Technical Lead), Elin Fosse (Lead Solution Architect), Aisha Siddiqui (Claims Hub Product Owner)
- Forum: Design Authority
- Supersedes: none
- Superseded by: none

## Context

Business rules such as referral cut-offs change on dates set by the business and the regulator, often between releases.

## Decision

Hold business rules as versioned configuration with effective dates instead of introducing a rules engine.

## Consequences

Thresholds such as fraud referral cut-offs live in application.yaml with effective dates.

## Notes

Questions about this decision go to the Design Authority agenda.
